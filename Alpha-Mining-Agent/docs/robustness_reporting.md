# Pre-specified robustness reporting (2026-10-18)

> English translation of two notes written after the design freeze. Part 1 was written before any formal run, part 2 before the test set was unlocked. **Neither changes the design:** the primary metrics P1 and P2, the pool-entry gates, the fitness and the mining loop follow [design_freeze.md](design_freeze.md). Both only add reporting.

## Part 1. Discrete vs. continuous factors (written before the formal runs)

### Classification rule

- Object: the **raw value** of a factor, i.e. the DSL output before winsorization and neutralization.
- Period: training (2016–2021).
- For each trading day, u_t = number of distinct finite values / number of finite values; days with fewer than 30 valid stocks are skipped.
- d = median of u_t over days. **d < 0.5 is discrete, otherwise continuous**; if d cannot be computed, the factor is discrete.
- Code: `alpha_mining/evaluation/robustness.py` (`DISCRETE_RATIO = 0.5`), with unit tests.

The threshold 0.5 was set before looking at the distribution of any factor.

### Result on Alpha101 (used only to check the rule; no IC was looked at)

21 of the 82 formulas are discrete, and d is clearly bimodal: the largest discrete value is 0.257 (#46), the smallest continuous value 0.503 (#73). A count by formula shape gave 22, one more than this rule. #73 is close to the threshold and is reported as a borderline case.

Discrete: #1, 4, 7, 21, 27, 43, 46, 61, 62, 64, 65, 68, 71, 74, 75, 81, 86, 92, 95, 96, 99.

### What is reported (after the formal runs)

1. P1 for each method × group, separately for discrete and continuous candidates (denominator: the valid candidates of that class generated by the method), together with the share of discrete candidates each method generates;
2. the number and share of discrete factors in the final qualified pool;
3. a note in the robustness and limitations section of the paper on the phenomenon (e.g. changing the industry of only two stocks moves the training IC of Alpha#65 from −0.0043 to +0.0042) and on how much the pool-entry gate is affected.

The main conclusions rest on the frozen primary metrics; the split is supplementary.

## Part 2. Sensitivity analysis: days with a defined IC (written before the test unlock)

> **The main table remains the result of the frozen pipeline and is not modified;** this part only adds a sensitivity analysis reported alongside it.

### The problem found

The frozen pipeline does not require a factor's IC to be defined on enough trading days. Near-constant factors (e.g. a short-window standard deviation of a step function such as `ts_std(ts_max(...), 3)`) are identical across the cross-section on most days, so their IC is undefined there and the ICIR comes from very few days and can be meaninglessly large (defined on only 2–9 training days, ICIR of 1–9).

In the formal runs only `gp1000_s2` is affected: 10 of its 57 qualified factors are of this kind, 4 of them entered the Top-10, and the GP search of that seed was pulled along by their inflated fitness. In every other run the largest |validation ICIR| among qualified factors is 0.254, with no such factor.

### Sensitivity rule

- A factor's IC-valid days = the number of trading days in the period on which the daily cross-sectional IC is defined (same definition as the IC itself: the last 6 days of each period are dropped for the label).
- **A factor is qualified only if its IC-valid days are at least 50% of the trading days in both the training and the validation period.** Qualification conditions 1–3 are otherwise unchanged.
- Under this rule, P1, the final qualified pool, the Top-10 and the composite are recomputed for **all runs** (exactly as in the main pipeline, with this one extra filter) and evaluated on the test periods in the same way (`scripts/build_sensitivity.py`, `results/final_sensitivity/`, `results/test_sensitivity/`).
- The 50% threshold was fixed before any test-period data were seen.

### Reporting

- The main table is unchanged; both sets of results are reported (`results/summary/sensitivity.csv`).
- The paper states the gap, the affected scope (only `gp1000_s2`), and that the search of that seed was itself affected and cannot be repaired by filtering afterwards.
